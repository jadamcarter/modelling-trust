title: "Trust Transport accepted in Ergo"
date: 2026-11-03
description: "How far should trust in an expert carry over when they speak about another field?"
---

"Trust Transport", by Nicolas Côté, J. Adam Carter and Emma C. Gordon, has been accepted for publication in *Ergo: An Open Access Journal of Philosophy*.

The paper asks how much of an expert's credibility in one field should carry over when they speak about another. Its answer is a rule for adjusting trust as an expert moves between fields, which turns on two things: how closely the two subject matters are related, and how broad a range of questions the expert has actually shown themselves to be reliable about. The rule bears on epistemic trespassing and on how broken trust can be rebuilt, and it's the model our workshops will test and extend over the coming year.

The abstract is on the [Publications]({{< relref "/publications" >}}) page, and a penultimate version will be posted there soon.
