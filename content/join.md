We're building a network of researchers who work on trust, expertise and models, in philosophy and beyond. Membership is informal and costs nothing.

## Mailing list

For news of workshops, talks and calls for papers, email us and ask to be added to the mailing list.

## Working with us

If you work on trust or expertise, in any discipline, and would like to speak, collaborate or develop a joint project, please get in touch. We're especially keen to hear from economists, psychologists, political scientists, computer scientists and health researchers, and from early-career researchers and PGRs.
