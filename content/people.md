## Co-leads

The three co-leads share the project equally, and each leads one of the thematic workshops.

### J. Adam Carter

Professor of Philosophy at the University of Glasgow and a Director of COGITO Epistemology Research Centre. Works in social epistemology and the epistemology of trust, autonomy and testimony, and is the author of *A Telic Theory of Trust* (Oxford University Press, 2024). Principal Investigator of the ERC-funded KNOW-HOW project.

### Nicolas Côté

Lecturer in Philosophy at the University of Glasgow. Works across normative ethics, the philosophy of economics and political theory, and brings formal modelling into philosophical research. Leads the project's modelling methodology.

### Emma C. Gordon

Reader in Philosophy at the University of Glasgow. Works in applied epistemology and bioethics, including trust in medicine and the ethics of expert advice, and leads interdisciplinary research collaborations at COGITO. Anchors the project's work on monitoring and repairing trust.

## The network

We're building a network of researchers across philosophy, economics, psychology, political science, computing science and public health who work on trust and expertise. If that's you, [we'd like to hear from you]({{< relref "/join" >}}).
